Commercial Flight Operations & Revenue Analysis

PostgreSQL · Python · Power BI

7M+flight records
2.1Mflights modeled
269Mpassengers

U.S. airlines publish enormous amounts of operational data and almost no revenue data. I built a pipeline over the Bureau of Transportation Statistics record set to answer the operational questions directly, then layered a modeled revenue view on top so profitability and load factor could be examined alongside delays and cancellations.

  • Cleaned, transformed, validated, and indexed 7M+ BTS flight records in Python and PostgreSQL.
  • Built a modeled commercial layer covering roughly 2.1M flights and 269M passengers to estimate revenue, cost, profitability, load factor, and fare-class mix.
  • Wrote SQL reporting views for carrier, route, delay, cancellation, and financial analysis.
  • Delivered the results as a multi-page Power BI dashboard built for someone else to explore.

The operational figures come from published BTS data. The revenue and cost layer is modeled, not reported — it’s there to make the operational numbers interpretable, not to state what any airline actually earned.
